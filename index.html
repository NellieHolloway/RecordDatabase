    📐 How Values Are Estimated

    Figures are model estimates, not appraisals — a starting point, not a substitute for checking a specific pressing.

    • Baselined at Near Mint; use the Grade selector to re-value the whole collection for your copies' condition.
    • Calibrated against 2025 Discogs/eBay sold medians for a sample spanning every tier.
    • Driven by era, pressing type, format (multi-LP sets), and per-title demand — with the mega-seller effect applied (blockbusters like Rumours or Thriller are common, so they stay modest).
    • Genuine rarities (early-60s originals, box sets) carry the top of the range.
